# DAY1 Public Services V1.0

## Direction Feedback

A lightweight pre-construction direction check based on one real garden photo and the owner's stated needs.

Public entry:
https://dayi.homes/direction-feedback/

Typical output:
- one visual direction image;
- three concise design suggestions.

## Consultation

Used to clarify the problem, circulation, priorities and obvious risks before deeper design work.

## Skeleton Plan

Used to establish the basic spatial structure, functional zoning, key dimensions, drainage direction and construction priorities before detailed design.

## Boundary

Public service descriptions explain what the client receives. Internal prompts, quality rules, automation logic, scoring systems and operating methods are not public assets.
