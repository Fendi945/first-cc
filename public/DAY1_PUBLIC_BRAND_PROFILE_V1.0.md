# DAY1 Public Brand Profile V1.0

DAY1 / 大一造园 is a garden design practice focused on natural, restrained and livable outdoor spaces.

## Public expression

- The client’s life is the protagonist; design is support.
- Start from how people want to live, not from how many elements can be added.
- Leave space for time, growth, light and everyday use.
- Good design should still feel right after the novelty is gone.

## Visual identity

Wordmark: dayi
Accent: brick-red dot
Primary colors:
- #C6402F
- #222222
- #F8F6F3

The public brand layer is intentionally simple. Internal operating systems, product roadmaps and business strategy remain private.
