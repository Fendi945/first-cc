# DAY1 Public Design Principles V1.0

## Core position

A good garden should first be safe, usable and sustainable in daily life, then become beautiful.

## Public principles

- Respect the real site instead of forcing a style onto it.
- Structure, drainage, circulation and long-term use come before decoration.
- Leave enough empty space; natural does not mean adding more plants.
- Use light, shadow, material and proportion to create atmosphere.
- Prefer restrained, low-maintenance solutions.
- Keep buildings, entrances, boundaries and real spatial proportions honest.
- Avoid creating unnecessary maintenance anxiety for the owner.
- Design should support real life rather than compete with it.

## Aesthetic direction

Natural / restrained / warm / quiet / real / low-maintenance.

Public brand palette:
- Brick red: #C6402F
- Warm black: #222222
- Warm white: #F8F6F3
